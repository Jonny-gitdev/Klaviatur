Klaviatur is a privacy-friendly Android application designed for piano players and musicians to manage their repertoire, practice sessions, and chord sheets.

Features:
• Repertoire Management: Track pieces you are learning and have mastered.
• Practice Sessions: Log and monitor your practice progress with built-in metronome.
• Sheet Music & Chord Library: View PDFs and ChordPro song sheets.
• Custom Lists: Organize your pieces into sets or categories.
• OpenOpus Integration: Easily search classical compositions and composers.
• Statistics: Visualize your practice habits over time.
• Local Backup: Easily backup and restore your database locally.
